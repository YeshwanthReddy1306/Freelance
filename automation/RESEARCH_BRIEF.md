# Research brief

The daily run reads this file before researching. Edit it to change what Claude looks for;
the next run picks the change up.

Why this changed (23 Nov 2026): about 70 offer-first emails to support/info/sales/press inboxes
produced zero human replies, only autoresponders, ticket numbers, bounces and declines. The
fixes below target the contact route, the copy and the volume. See `CLAUDE.md` email rules.

## Who to find

Small, founder-led brands where the owner or a small team reads the inbox: roughly under
$5M revenue, 1-15 people, an owner or founder named on the brand's own About or Contact page.
Shopify preferred, but any real online store is fine if the problem is visible. Local and
niche businesses (tea, candles, skincare, leather, food, hobby gear, local services) are
welcome. Considered-purchase products with many near-identical options are best.

Do NOT target mid-size funded brands with in-house growth teams or agencies (that is where the
blanket "no solicitation" autoresponders came from).

## What to offer them

First touch makes NO offer. It names one specific, verified problem on their live site and asks
one small question (who looks after the website?) and offers a screenshot. The tool or ad offer
(`CLAUDE.md` A/B/C) only comes after a person replies.

## Signals to look for

A problem you can see yourself on the live site and describe in one hedged sentence: a crowded
option list with no comparison, sets that don't say what is inside, an all-products page that
mixes unrelated items, zero-price or placeholder products, a confusing size/compatibility
choice, no gift builder. Note the URL and what you saw. Re-open the page before drafting.

## Avoid

Support, info, sales, help, care, orders and customer-service inboxes (they go to ticket
systems and bots). Mid-size or funded brands, enterprise brands, Amazon-first or wholesale-only
brands, brands outside the US unless the owner is clearly reachable, and anything in
`data/ledger.csv` or `data/do_not_contact.csv`.

## Per day

- Candidates to research: 15
- Drafts to produce: at most 5 (quality over volume)
